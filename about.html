Why abacus

Why learn maths with an abacus?

Learning that children enjoy

Learning mental arithmetic through the image of an abacus breaks away from one-way maths teaching. Flexible, hands-on methods spark children's interest and grow their desire to learn more.

Both sides of the brain, working together

The left brain handles numbers and logic; the right brain handles pictures, space and creativity. Children move beads with both hands and later picture the abacus in their minds, so both sides train together. That coordination is what drives strong problem-solving and creative thinking.

Best learning age
4–14

The best years to start

A child's brain grows fastest between the ages of 4 and 14. Imagination is rich and learning is quick in these years, so we recommend starting before 15.
