Abacus Mental Arithmetic

The 8-level programme

Levels & duration

Each level is one term. Levels 1–5 have 12 classes over 3 months; levels 6–8 have 16 classes over 4 months. Every class is 2 hours, once a week on Sunday, so the programme never clashes with school timings or curriculum.

What your child will learn

  1. Recognise and understand the abacus
  2. Count using beads
  3. Move beads with both hands
  4. Correct posture and hand position
  5. Turn numbers into beads and beads into numbers
  6. Add and subtract without carrying
  7. Add and subtract with carrying
  8. Master the rules of addition and subtraction
  9. Multiplication and division
  10. Calculate on an imaginary abacus
  11. Use listening and visualising for speed and accuracy
Complete all 8 levels to get the full benefit. Progress after the first few levels is exciting, but it is not the peak.

Guidelines

For students & parents

Students

  • Practise at home for 10–15 minutes every day
  • Time yourself and note your speed
  • Write exercises neatly and clearly
  • Complete home practice on time
  • Concentrate on every dictated sum

Parents

  • Help your child attend every class on time
  • Encourage 10–15 minutes of willing home practice daily
  • Let the instructor guide the method in class
  • Parents are not permitted inside the classroom
  • Check your child's progress at least once a month
